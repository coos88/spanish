# Chapter 2: Daily life and plans

Talk about your daily routine, make plans, say what hurts and compare things, using the irregular present tense and object pronouns.

**At the end you can:**
- Describe your daily routine from getting up to going to bed
- Make, accept and refuse a plan with a friend (ir a, tener que, querer, poder)
- Tell a doctor what hurts and understand simple advice
- Buy clothes in your size and compare two items

## Lesson order
1. **Stem-changing verbs**: pienso, duermo, pido: the vowel in the stem changes, except for nosotros and vosotros.
2. **Verbs irregular only in yo**: hago, pongo, salgo, traigo, sé, veo, doy, conozco: one odd form, the rest is regular.
3. **Reflexive verbs and daily routine**: me levanto, te duchas, se acuesta: the pronoun goes before the verb and matches the subject.
4. **Plans, obligations and wants**: ir a, tener que, deber, querer, poder + infinitive: the second verb never changes.
5. **What is happening now: estar + gerund**: estoy comiendo, está lloviendo: -ando / -iendo for actions in progress right now.
6. **Direct object pronouns: lo, la, los, las**: Lo tengo, la veo, quiero comprarlos: 'it, him, her, them' go before the verb or stick to the infinitive.
7. **Indirect objects and the gustar family**: me, te, le, nos, os, les: to whom. Me gusta, me encanta, me duele all use them.
8. **This, that and comparing things**: este, ese, aquel; más/menos ... que; mejor, peor, mayor, menor.
