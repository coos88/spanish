# Chapter 1: Survive and introduce yourself

Pronounce what you read, build simple sentences in the present tense, and handle first meetings, cafés and directions.

**At the end you can:**
- Greet people and introduce yourself
- Say where you are from, your age and what you do
- Order food and drink and ask the price
- Ask where things are and tell the time

## Lesson order
1. **Sounds, stress and accent marks**: Spanish is spelled as it sounds. Learn the rules once.
2. **Gender and articles**: Every noun is masculine or feminine. Learn the article with the word.
3. **Plurals and adjectives**: Adjectives agree with the noun and follow it.
4. **Present tense and tú / usted**: Regular verbs: drop -ar/-er/-ir and add the endings.
5. **ser vs estar**: Two verbs for "to be".
6. **tener and hay**: Having, ages, feelings, and "there is / there are".
7. **Negation and questions**: Put no before the verb. Question words always take an accent.
8. **Numbers, time and days**: Count to 100, tell the time, name the days.
9. **gustar: liking things**: The thing you like is the subject; the verb agrees with it.
10. **Possessives and little words**: my, your, our, and the prepositions you need to say where things are.
