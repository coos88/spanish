# Chapter 3: Tell what happened

Talk about yesterday, last week and your holidays: past events, background description, and a story that hangs together.

**At the end you can:**
- Say what you did yesterday, last weekend or on holiday, in the right past tense
- Report something recent or today's news with the present perfect (he perdido, hemos comido)
- Describe how things were in the past: the weather, a place, a person, a habit
- Tell a short story in order (primero, luego, al final) and ask for or give simple directions

## Lesson order
1. **Past time markers**: ayer, anoche, la semana pasada, hace + time, ya / todavía no
2. **Preterite: regular verbs**: hablé, comí, viví: finished events in the past
3. **Eight key irregular preterites**: fui, hice, tuve, estuve, pude, di, vi: no accents
4. **Present perfect: he hecho**: he, has, ha, hemos, habéis, han + participle; Spain uses it for today and this week
5. **Imperfect: setting the scene**: era, tenía, estaba, había, hacía: description, states and habits
6. **Connectors: telling a story**: primero, luego, después, entonces, pero, porque, mientras, al final
7. **Directions and polite requests**: perdone, dígame, espere, mire, siga, gire, tome, cruce + hay que
